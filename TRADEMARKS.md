# Trademarks and official builds

The MPL-2.0 and Apache-2.0 licenses grant copyright and patent permissions;
they do not grant trademark rights.

`BIM Explorer` is the descriptive open-source project and product name. Forks
may accurately describe their origin and compatibility, but must not imply
that a modified build is an official `menaje` release.

`Coni`, `Coni Spatial`, their logos, and the `menaje` publisher identity are
commercial product and distribution identifiers. They are not licensed for a
fork's product branding. References needed to describe interoperability or
origin remain allowed.

Official Community binaries are only the assets attached to releases at
<https://github.com/menaje/bim-explorer/releases>. Coni Spatial packages,
updates, support, Workspace authority, and verified delivery are separate from
the BIM Explorer Community release channel.
